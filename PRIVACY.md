# Chai Connect privacy policy

Chai Connect is run by the ACM-W student chapter at FAST-NUCES Karachi for the Chai Connect 2026 event.

## Google Drive access

The Chai Connect app connects to one Google Drive: the ACM-W organiser's own account. It uses that
access only to save the photos and videos that attendees choose to share on the "Share the moment"
page, into the organiser's Share the moment folders. It does not read, change or share any other file.
No attendee is ever asked to sign in with Google, and attendees never get access to the Drive.

## What we collect when you share a photo or video

- The photo or video you choose, stored unchanged in the organiser's Google Drive.
- The name you type, or "Anonymous" if you choose that.
- A random device ID in a cookie, so the same file isn't saved twice and uploads can't be abused.
  Only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is stored.

We don't sell or share this with anyone outside the ACM-W organising team. Shared moments may be
shown at the event or in ACM-W's event recap.

## Removal

To have a photo or video removed, contact the ACM-W FAST-NUCES Karachi team and tell us roughly when
you uploaded it and the name you used.
